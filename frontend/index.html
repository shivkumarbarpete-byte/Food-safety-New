About FoodSafetyHub

An innovative MCA 1st year project developed at IIIT Bhopal to revolutionize food safety testing

Heuristic Analysis

Comprehensive evaluation of food products based on multiple safety parameters including ingredients, expiry, storage conditions, and FSSAI compliance.

ML Predictions

Advanced k-NN machine learning algorithm to predict food safety based on pH, moisture, and temperature parameters.

Statistical Testing

Z-test and T-test implementations for laboratory data analysis with configurable confidence levels.

      Scope & Technical Limitations
      
        
      Transparent operational boundaries, data boundaries, and application scope
      
      
      
      
      
        
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
      Preliminary Screening Scope
      
            
      
            
      
              FoodSafetyHub provides preliminary risk assessments based on user-supplied information, observable storage conditions, and open barcode databases. It serves as an informative decision-support tool.
            
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
      Product Data Origins
      
            
      
            
      
              Barcode scanners fetch available product metadata from open repositories (Open Food Facts). Data retrieved reflects database entries and does not constitute laboratory safety verification.
            
      
          
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
      Experimental ML Estimates
      
            
      
            
      
              Machine learning models (k-NN) generate preliminary estimations based on training feature vectors (pH, moisture, temperature). ML outputs are supplementary estimates and cannot replace official food testing.
            
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
      Laboratory Statistical Tools
      
            
      
            
      
              Laboratory statistical modules perform standard statistical computations (Z-test/T-test) on user-entered measurements. Statistical significance confirms measurement properties against specified reference limits, but does not equal complete microbial or chemical safety certification.
            
      
          
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
          
      Official Regulatory & Certification Boundaries
      
        
      
        
        
          
      • Authoritative Standards: Reference limits and safety parameters must come from official regulatory standards (e.g. FSSAI manuals) provided by the user.
        • 
          
      • Certification Scope: Official food safety certification, regulatory compliance enforcement, and legal accreditation are outside the technical scope of this academic application.
